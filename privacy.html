Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how personal information is handled when you visit fortmilo.co.uk or contact FortMilo Lab by email.

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Who controls your information

Controller: Luca Pacini trading as FortMilo Lab, England, United Kingdom.

Contact: info@fortmilo.co.uk.

Information collected

This website does not provide user accounts, payment processing, advertising analytics or a contact form.

FortMilo Lab may process:

  • your name, email address, organisation and the content of messages you send by email;
  • records needed to respond to, secure and administer an enquiry or business relationship;
  • technical connection information processed by the website host, which may include an IP address, request information, browser or device information and security logs.

Purposes and lawful bases

Email-enquiry information is used to respond to you, assess a requested business or technical interaction, maintain relevant records and protect FortMilo Lab's legal and security interests.

The usual lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to requested communications and operating a secure professional practice. Information may also be processed where necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, perform a contract or comply with a legal obligation.

Website hosting and recipients

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request and security information when serving the site. GitHub's handling of personal information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email and domain-service providers may process information needed to deliver and secure email. Information is not sold and is not shared for unrelated marketing. It may be disclosed to professional advisers, service providers or public authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required.

International transfers

GitHub and other technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where UK personal information is transferred internationally, the relevant provider is responsible for the transfer mechanisms and safeguards described in its contractual and privacy documentation.

Retention

Email enquiries and related business correspondence are normally retained for 24 months after the last substantive contact, then deleted unless a longer period is needed for an active relationship, a legal obligation, security investigation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Technical hosting logs are controlled and retained by GitHub under GitHub's own policies and operational requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • request deletion or restriction;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • request portability where it applies; and
  • withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.

To exercise a right, email info@fortmilo.co.uk. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed.

Complaints

You may complain directly to FortMilo Lab using the contact address above. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. See ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, service providers or processing activities change. The current version and update date will remain published on this page.
