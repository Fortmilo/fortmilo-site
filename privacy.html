Provider arrangements and locations may change, so this notice does not claim that all processing remains in the United Kingdom.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To exercise a right or raise a privacy concern, email info@fortmilo.co.uk. You also have the right to make a data-protection complaint to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
