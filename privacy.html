Privacy

Privacy notice.

How personal data is handled when you visit fortmilo.co.uk or contact FortMilo.

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Luca Pacini trading as FortMilo Lab, using FortMilo as the public brand. Contact: info@fortmilo.co.uk. Postal address is available on request pending final business-address confirmation.

Information processed

FortMilo does not operate website analytics, advertising trackers, account registration or a contact form. The site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub’s own privacy terms govern that hosting-layer processing.

When you email FortMilo, the information in your email, your email address and related correspondence are processed so the enquiry can be answered and, where relevant, a business relationship can be managed.

Purpose and lawful basis

Email correspondence is processed for the legitimate interests of responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and protecting legal rights. FortMilo does not use contact details for automated profiling or advertising.

Sharing and international processing

Email and website-hosting providers may process technical or correspondence data as service providers. Their infrastructure may involve processing outside the United Kingdom under their own contractual safeguards and privacy terms. Personal data is not sold.

Retention

General enquiries are normally retained for up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact. Records may be kept longer where needed for an active business relationship, legal obligation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, object to processing, or request a portable copy. Email info@fortmilo.co.uk to make a request.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

This notice will be updated before FortMilo introduces any materially different data collection, analytics or contact process.
