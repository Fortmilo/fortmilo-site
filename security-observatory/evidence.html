Evidence & Coverage

Availability, outcome and coverage stay separate.

Security Benchmark for Salesforce

Traceability, not certification

Security Observatory references Security Benchmark for Salesforce control identifiers and preserves retained outcome context: Risk evidence, No risk evidence surfaced, Unknown/Error or Not assessed.

It does not assert that a control is satisfied, certify an organisation or provide a compliance score.
