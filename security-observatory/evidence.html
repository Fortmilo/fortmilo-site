Evidence & Coverage

Availability, coverage and result are separate.

Missing or incomplete evidence must never be mistaken for a pass.

Coverage and result

Benchmark coverage is not scanner health

Coverage: Automated, Partial Evidence, Manual Required, Not Covered or Extended Check.

Latest result: Not Evaluated, Pass, Fail, Unknown/Error or Manual Required. Unknown/Error never counts as Pass.

SBS alignment supports evidence review. It is not certification or formal compliance scoring.
