SBS & Evidence

Availability, coverage and result are separate.

The Observatory separates scanner availability from benchmark coverage and latest result so missing evidence cannot be mistaken for a pass.

Three evidence axes

  • Scanner availabilityDid the scanner return usable evidence?
  • SBS coverageHow far can the Observatory evidence the control?
  • Latest resultWhat did the latest completed evidence show?

Scanner availability

Evidence returned, zero observed or not assessed

Evidence returned

The scanner family returned usable evidence at the configured level.

Zero observed

The scanner completed and explicitly observed zero matching records.

Not assessed — reason

The source was unavailable, blocked or incomplete, with the next safe action shown.

SBS coverage

Coverage without a compliance claim

  • AutomatedThe Observatory directly checks the control.
  • Partial EvidenceUseful evidence exists but cannot fully prove the control.
  • Manual RequiredGovernance, process or external evidence is required.
  • Not CoveredThe current implementation does not cover the control.
  • Extended CheckAn Observatory check has no direct SBS control mapping.
