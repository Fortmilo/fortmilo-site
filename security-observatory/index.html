Product boundaries

  • Separated runtime boundariesCollection and retention occur inside the subscriber Salesforce organisation. The LWC prepares the allow-listed CSV in the authenticated browser session; downloading it creates a file outside Salesforce.
  • Read-only assessmentThe Observatory is advisory. It does not remediate or modify the Salesforce security configuration it assesses. Product-owned records retain scan results, evidence, findings and comparison data.
  • Evidence-boundedUnavailable sources and validation limits remain visible.
  • Sandbox-firstNot yet available for public installation.

Licence assignment evidence: in V1, each scan retains up to 1,000 assignment evidence records for each of the three licence families — Package Licences, Permission Set Licences and Salesforce User Licences — so at most 3,000 across those families per scan. This limits retained evidence only; it does not limit how many Salesforce users, licences or assignments your org can have. Where the full population cannot be retained safely, that evidence is reported as Incomplete rather than shown as complete.

Assignment identities are Everything-only. The 3,000-record maximum is theoretical; actual safe capture may be lower — including zero — because Salesforce transaction and DML headroom must be preserved. A zero captured count does not mean zero assignments and cannot be Complete; an unknown expected count remains unknown. Licence-capacity totals and Balanced holding summaries are separate from individual assignment evidence, and capped retained counts are not organisation totals.
