Product focus

OAuth-first, evidence-bounded

The Overview leads with OAuth grants requiring review. A retained authorisation record is not described as a live or usable credential, and credentials are never tested by attempting use.

Unavailable evidence is shown as Not assessed — reason with a safe next action. Health Check remains scoreless supporting evidence rather than a product headline.
