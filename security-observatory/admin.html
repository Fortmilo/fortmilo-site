Admin

Safe controls around one complete scan plan.

Control evidence depth, completed-scan retention, scheduled execution, stale-lock recovery and the local SBS registry.

Admin boundaries

  • Permission gatedSettings and recovery require explicit Observatory administration access.
  • Disabled by defaultScheduled scans use fixed Weekly or Monthly presets.
  • No free-form CRONThe first scheduling slice avoids arbitrary scheduling input.
  • No remediationControls manage Observatory execution and evidence only.

Evidence levels

Depth, not scanner selection

Manual and scheduled scans use the same Apex-owned scanner plan. Top Issues, Balanced and Everything change what is retained, displayed, compared and exported.

Operations

Bounded execution and recovery

Scheduled scans

Admin-managed, disabled by default and restricted to Weekly or Monthly presets.

Retention

Completed-scan maximum 1–10, with edition-aware defaults and a Developer Edition Everything cap of one.

Stale-lock recovery

Confirmed orphan scans can be closed to Failed without deleting evidence or cancelling active jobs.
