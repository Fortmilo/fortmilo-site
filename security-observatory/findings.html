Findings session

Triage first. Detail on demand.

The Findings session groups retained evidence into operator-relevant areas, supports filtering and opens full detail without flooding the main view.

Finding detail

  • Evidence summaryWhat was observed and why it requires review.
  • Source and limitationWhere the evidence came from and what it cannot prove.
  • Safe verificationA bounded route to verify the condition in Salesforce.
  • Advisory reviewReview state only; no security setting is changed.

Finding workflow

What an operator can do

Filter and prioritise

Focus retained findings by severity, area, review state and supporting SBS or Extended Check context.

Open safe detail

Inspect evidence, source, limitation, affected context and a safe verification route.

Record advisory review

Track operational follow-up without changing Salesforce permissions, sessions, applications or endpoints.

Connected application evidence

Evidence passport, not a risk score

The Connected App Evidence Passport brings together safe retained application identity, governance context, usage evidence, linked findings and explicit evidence gaps.

It does not claim OAuth scope visibility where scopes are unavailable, does not infer credential usability and does not produce a confident low-risk state without the required policy evidence.

Evidence boundary

Only supported evidence becomes a finding

A missing source, blocked query or unavailable detail does not become a zero-risk result. It becomes a visible not-assessed or Unknown/Error state with cause, limitation and next action.

The application never verifies credentials by replaying them and does not describe an OAuth authorisation record as a live or usable token.
