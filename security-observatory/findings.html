Findings

Triage first. Detail on demand.

Filter retained findings, then open only the evidence and verification context needed for review.

Finding detail

  • Evidence summaryWhat was observed and why it requires review.
  • Source and limitationWhere the evidence came from and what it cannot prove.
  • Safe verificationA bounded route to verify the condition in Salesforce.
  • Advisory reviewReview state only; no security setting is changed.

Finding workflow

Prioritise and inspect

Filter and prioritise

Focus retained findings by severity, area, review state and supporting SBS or Extended Check context.

Open safe detail

Inspect the evidence summary, source, limitation, affected context and safe Salesforce verification route.

Record advisory review

Track operational follow-up without changing Salesforce permissions, sessions, applications or endpoints.

Evidence boundary

Missing evidence is not a clean result

A blocked query, unavailable source or missing retained detail becomes a visible not-assessed or Unknown/Error state with cause, limitation and next action.

Credentials are never replayed, and retained OAuth authorisation records are not described as live or usable tokens.
