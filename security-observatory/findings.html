Findings

Triage first. Evidence on demand.

Filter retained findings, then open only the evidence and verification context needed for review.

Finding detail

  • Evidence summaryWhat was observed and why it requires review.
  • Source and limitationWhere the evidence came from and what it cannot prove.
  • Safe verificationA bounded route to verify the condition in Salesforce.
  • Advisory reviewReview state only; no Salesforce security setting is changed.

Finding workflow

Prioritise, inspect, record

Severity first

Focus Critical, High and Moderate findings without turning unknown evidence into a lower-risk statement.

Open safe detail

Inspect evidence summary, source, limitation, affected context and a safe Salesforce verification route.

Record advisory review

Track operational follow-up without changing permissions, sessions, applications or endpoints.

Evidence boundary

Missing evidence is not a clean result

A blocked query, unavailable source or incomplete retained detail becomes a visible Not assessed — reason or Unknown/Error state with cause, limitation and next action.

OAuth authorisation records are described as retained grants requiring review. The application does not test credential usability.
