Findings

Triage first. Detail on demand.

Filter retained findings, inspect safe evidence and record advisory review without changing Salesforce security settings.

Finding detail

  • Evidence summaryWhat was observed and why it requires review.
  • Source and limitationWhere the evidence came from and what it cannot prove.
  • Safe verificationA bounded route to verify the condition in Salesforce.
  • Advisory reviewReview state only; no security setting is changed.

Workflow

Prioritise, inspect, review

Filter and prioritise

Focus retained findings by severity, area, review state and SBS or Extended Check context.

Open safe detail

Inspect evidence, source, limitation, affected context and verification route.

Record review

Track follow-up without changing users, permissions, sessions, applications or endpoints.

Evidence boundary

Only supported evidence becomes a finding

A missing source, blocked query or unavailable detail does not become a zero-risk result. It becomes a visible not-assessed or Unknown/Error state with cause, limitation and next action.

OAuth authorisation records do not prove credential usability, and the application never verifies credentials by replaying them.
