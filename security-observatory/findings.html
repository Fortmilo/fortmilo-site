Findings

Triage first. Detail on demand.

Findings group retained risk evidence by security question, with source, limitation, verification guidance and advisory review state.

Finding detail

  • Evidence summaryWhat was observed and why it requires review.
  • Source and limitationWhere evidence came from and what it cannot prove.
  • Safe verificationA bounded route to verify the condition in Salesforce.
  • Advisory reviewReview state only; no security setting is changed.

Evidence terminology

Separate severity, outcome and coverage

The approved evidence vocabulary keeps these axes separate rather than presenting a binary control result.
